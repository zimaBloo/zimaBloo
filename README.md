## Hi, I'm Igor

I'm an AI engineer in Helsinki. I work as a research assistant in AI engineering at the University of Helsinki, and I'm doing my master's in Computer Science there (finishing in 2027).

Most of what I build now is AI systems, lately with AI coding agents. One model plans the work and another reviews it. Then I go through whatever's left before it goes in. I'll put more of it here as it gets built.

### What I've worked on

The research job is my first one in AI, since October 2026. Before that I made an IoT security app: a small Python program that runs a CatBoost model to sort IoT network traffic and flag attacks. [See the code](https://github.com/zimaBloo/IOT-SECURITY-deployment)

### Before AI

I started out in software engineering and security. I have a BSc from LUT University (with distinction), and my thesis was on how blockchain bridges get hacked. It got a 5 out of 5. I also led the IT and support team for Pirates of the Baltic Sea, a student event for 1,500 people.

### About the game

The two gloves at the top are a small neural network. It learns from every game people play against it. Mostly it's there for fun. [How it works](https://github.com/zimaBloo/beat-my-ai)
